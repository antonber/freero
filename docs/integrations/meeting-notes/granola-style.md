# Q4 onboarding review

Thu, Oct 29, 2026 · Priya Raman, Tom Okafor, Lena Fischer

The team looked at where new workspaces stall in their first week and agreed to ship a lighter
first-run flow before the holiday freeze.

### Where people drop off
- Half of new workspaces never invite a second person
- The template picker is the slowest screen (median 41 s)
  - Most people scroll past the first row without picking
- Mobile sign-ups rarely come back on desktop

### Ideas we liked
- A "start with your team" step right after sign-up
- Fewer templates on the first screen, grouped by job

### Decisions
- Ship the three-step first-run flow behind a flag for 20% of new workspaces
- Drop the video tour from onboarding
- Keep the template picker, but show six templates instead of twenty
